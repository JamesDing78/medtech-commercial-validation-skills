# Monetization Strategy

This repository follows an open-core model.

## Free Core

The public skills provide reusable methodology, evidence classification, customer ROI logic, risk gates, and role-binding examples. They are distributed under the MIT License.

## Paid Offers

Revenue should come from services and products built around the skills:

1. Industry template packs for hospital access, rehabilitation, sports health, schools, and embodied intelligence.
2. Paid commercial-validation sprints covering customer evidence, ROI, transaction design, compliance boundaries, and paid-pilot design.
3. Enterprise private deployment with permissions, internal evidence, financial models, audit logs, and training.
4. Annual updates, customer success, regulatory evidence refresh, and implementation support.
5. A hosted SaaS or GitHub App only after repeated paid delivery proves that the workflow is stable.

## Pricing Discipline

The repository does not publish invented market prices. Each offer should be priced from delivery cost, research complexity, compliance review, software cost, support load, acquisition cost, risk reserve, and a target margin.

Initial prices are internal hypotheses until validated by formal quotes, paid pilots, delivery time records, and renewal evidence.

## Data Boundary

Do not sell raw patient, student, member, biometric, or medical records. Charge for compliant analysis, reporting, model support, workflow implementation, and software access only when the relevant authorization, security, and contractual conditions are satisfied.

## Recommended Sequence

Free public skill -> paid validation sprint -> reusable templates -> enterprise implementation -> recurring support -> SaaS or GitHub App.

Do not build a complex platform before there are repeated paid workflows and evidence of renewal.
