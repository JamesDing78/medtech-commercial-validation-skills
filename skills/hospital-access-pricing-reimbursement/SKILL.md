# Hospital Access Pricing Reimbursement

## Workflow
1. Separate fixed equipment, consumables, software, service fees, maintenance, and clinical service charges.
2. Check national and provincial policy: medical service price items, DRG/DIP, sunshine procurement, consumable coding, and GPO history.
3. Build hospital access paths by buyer: equipment department, rehabilitation department, research lab, school, community center, and internet hospital.
4. Tie pricing to procurement route and department economics.

## Guardrails
- Do not assume equipment itself is reimbursed by医保.
- Mark data that requires platform login or hospital/channel confirmation.
