# Record & Replay Sales Review

## Workflow
1. Record the interaction: customer, scene, objective, attendees, questions, objections, demo content, and next action.
2. Tag the replay: pain category, purchase stage, regulatory concern, technical concern, service issue, and champion strength.
3. Feed outputs into CRM-like pipeline, case library, service issue log, and financial model conversion assumptions.

## Guardrails
- Obtain consent before recording meetings, demos, patients, students, or minors.
- Store personal and health-related data under least-necessary access rules.
