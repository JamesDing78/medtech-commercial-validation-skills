# Customer ROI And Finance Rules

## Customer-First Model

For each customer type calculate:

`customer contribution = approved service contribution + efficiency value + recurring service value + research/quality value - direct delivery cost`

`customer payback = customer investment / monthly customer contribution`

Keep research, reputation, recruitment, and data-quality value as separate non-cash or conditional value. Do not silently convert them into cash revenue.

## Company Revenue Separation

Model independently:

- equipment sale: quantity × net selling price;
- operating lease: active leased units × months × rent, with deposit excluded from revenue;
- financing: documented commission or spread only;
- subscription: attached accounts × fee plus eligible renewals;
- report/project: paid reports or projects × unit price;
- maintenance/SLA: eligible installed accounts × annual fee;
- channel/joint operation: contract-defined net share after refunds and direct delivery cost;
- data service: contract revenue from an authorized data product or analytics service.

Do not double count first-year software included in equipment or lease contracts. Do not count uncontracted data value as profit.

## Variable Governance

Each variable must state its formula, scope, units, editable range, source, and what it does not affect. Typical variables include:

`sale discount`, `sale units`, `leased units/utilization`, `project count`, `subscription attach rate`, `renewal rate`, `paid reports`, `assessment fee`, `maintenance attach rate`, `direct service cost`, `channel share`, and `collection delay`.

Cash flow must show collection timing, lease asset investment, deposits, working capital, warranty reserve, service labor, travel, spare parts, and bad debt separately from profit.

## Scenario Discipline

Maintain three layers:

1. Current formula model: the only operating forecast.
2. Historical snapshots: preserved for comparison, never presented as current commitments.
3. Pressure scenarios: delayed deals, price decline, volume decline, lease default, renewal decline, service-cost increase, regulatory-use restriction, data incident, and combined stress.
