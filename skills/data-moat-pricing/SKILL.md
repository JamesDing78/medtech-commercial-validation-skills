# Data-Moat Pricing

## Workflow
1. Define the data asset: modality, sampling frequency, label quality, clinical/field context, longitudinal depth, consent status, and exclusivity.
2. Separate products into hardware sale, software subscription, assessment report, data analytics, training/support, and annual maintenance.
3. Score the moat from 1-5 on scarcity, cost to reproduce, regulatory defensibility, clinical relevance, interoperability, and customer lock-in.
4. Build three pricing cases: entry validation, institutional deployment, and platform/data service.
5. Tie every price to buyer value: department revenue, research output, operational efficiency, risk reduction, or downstream service income.

## Guardrails
- Do not price regulated clinical claims without stating the intended use and compliance boundary.
- Do not treat personal health data as a monetizable asset unless consent, privacy, retention, and cross-border rules are addressed.
- Keep assumptions editable in the financial model.
