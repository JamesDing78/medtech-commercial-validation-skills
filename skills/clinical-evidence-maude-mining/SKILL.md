# Clinical Evidence And MAUDE Mining

## Workflow
1. Identify comparable devices by function and intended use.
2. Collect public clinical studies, regulatory summaries, labels, contraindications, and limitations.
3. Search MAUDE by product code, brand, manufacturer, and generic device name.
4. Convert evidence into clinical pain points, risk controls, technical barriers, and claims boundaries.

## Caveats
MAUDE reports are passive surveillance data and cannot be used as incidence rates without denominators.
