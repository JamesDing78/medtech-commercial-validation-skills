# Market Pain Mining

## Workflow
1. Segment customers by workflow, not just institution type.
2. For each segment capture pain, current workaround, purchase trigger, decision maker, budget source, evidence needed, and likely objection.
3. Score pain by urgency, willingness to pay, regulatory friction, deployment complexity, and repeatability.
4. Convert high-scoring pains into interview questions and pilot success metrics.

## Output Format
Use a pain map with columns: segment, scene, pain, evidence, buyer, budget, solution package, proof needed, next validation.
