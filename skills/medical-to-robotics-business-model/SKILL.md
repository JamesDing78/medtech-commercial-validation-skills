# Medical-to-Robotics Business Model

## Three Stages
1. Medical/research wedge: build credibility through hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and sports science labs.
2. Replicable service network: convert the wedge into county medical communities, schools, community rehab, and recurring assessment workflows.
3. Robotics and personal platform: use validated motion data, scenarios, and service workflows to extend into robot training, remote rehab, and personal health management.

## Required Outputs
- Segment map, buyer map, decision chain, budget source, channel motion, and proof points.
- Milestones for 3 months, 12 months, 36 months, and 5 years.
- What must remain non-medical until regulatory claims are cleared.
