# Medical-to-Robotics Business Model

## Three Stages
1. Medical/research wedge: build credibility through hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and sports science labs.
2. Replicable service network: convert the wedge into county medical communities, schools, community rehab, and recurring assessment workflows.
3. Robotics and personal platform: use validated motion data, scenarios, and service workflows to extend into robot training, remote rehab, and personal health management.

## Required Outputs
- Segment map, buyer map, decision chain, budget source, channel motion, and proof points.
- Milestones for 3 months, 12 months, 36 months, and 5 years.
- What must remain non-medical until regulatory claims are cleared.

# 中文双语参考 / Chinese-English Reference

## 适用说明 / Purpose

用于将医工交叉机会拆解为分阶段商业模式，尤其适用于从临床康复、运动生物力学延伸到机器人、具身智能和个人健康平台的业务规划。 / Use this skill to decompose medical-engineering crossover opportunities into staged business models, especially from clinical rehabilitation and sports biomechanics toward robotics, embodied intelligence, and personal health platforms.

## 三条业务线 / Three Business Lines

1. **医疗与科研切入线 / Medical and research wedge:** 通过医院、康复中心和体育科学实验室建立可信度。 / Build credibility through hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and sports science labs.
2. **可复制服务网络线 / Replicable service network:** 将切入业务转化为县域医共体、学校、社区康复和持续性评估流程。 / Convert the wedge into county medical communities, schools, community rehabilitation, and recurring assessment workflows.
3. **机器人与个人健康平台线 / Robotics and personal health platform:** 使用经过验证的运动数据、应用场景和服务流程，延伸到机器人训练、远程康复和个人健康管理。 / Use validated motion data, scenarios, and service workflows to extend into robot training, remote rehabilitation, and personal health management.

## 必须输出 / Required Outputs

- 分群地图、买方地图、决策链、预算来源、渠道动作和证明点。 / Segment map, buyer map, decision chain, budget source, channel motion, and proof points.
- 3个月、12个月、36个月和5年的里程碑。 / Milestones for 3 months, 12 months, 36 months, and 5 years.
- 在监管声明获得明确批准前，哪些内容必须保持为非医疗用途。 / What must remain non-medical until regulatory claims are cleared.
