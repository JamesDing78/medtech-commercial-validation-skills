# Interactive Pitch Deck

## Required Sections
- Executive thesis and 3-month validation plan.
- Medical GTM path and sports education GTM path.
- Scene/package selector.
- Compliance pathway selector.
- Financial scenario toggle.
- After-sales service and team scaling plan.
- Evidence, source, and uncertainty panel.

## Rules
- Build the working experience directly; avoid a generic landing page.
- Make assumptions visible and editable where practical.
- Do not hide regulatory or reimbursement uncertainty.
