# Commercialization Metrics

## Funnel

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Qualified lead | Named organization with a relevant workflow and decision owner |
| Evidence interview | Structured conversation with recorded pain, workaround, budget, and objection |
| Proposal | Formal scope, price, timing, acceptance, and exclusions sent to a buyer |
| Paid pilot | Signed or otherwise documented paid delivery with defined acceptance |
| Renewal | Customer purchases an additional period, scope, or service |

## Economics

```text
conversion_rate = paid_pilots / qualified_leads
delivery_gross_margin = (revenue - direct_delivery_cost) / revenue
renewal_rate = renewed_customers / customers_due_for_renewal
customer_payback = customer_investment / verified_customer_benefit_per_period
```

## Minimum Review Set

Every month record:

- qualified leads;
- evidence interviews;
- proposals and proposal value;
- paid pilots and cash collected;
- delivery hours and direct cost;
- customer outcome and renewal signal;
- compliance or data incidents;
- reusable assets created.

Do not report downloads, stars, or market size as revenue or customer demand.
