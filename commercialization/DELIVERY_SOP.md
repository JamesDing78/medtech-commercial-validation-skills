# Delivery SOP

## Stage 1: Qualify

Complete the customer intake. Reject requests that have no decision owner, no measurable problem, or require unauthorized data or medical claims.

## Stage 2: Scope

Define intended use, users, workflow, deliverables, evidence sources, data boundaries, acceptance metrics, and out-of-scope work.

## Stage 3: Quote

Separate base delivery, optional research, compliance review, on-site work, support, tax, and pass-through costs. Do not bundle unknown work into an unlimited fixed fee.

## Stage 4: Deliver

Keep a record of source, date, assumption, customer confirmation, work hours, changes, and unresolved risks. Use the specialist Skills only when the decision requires them.

## Stage 5: Review

Run an independent review of evidence, calculations, claims, data handling, empty states, and acceptance criteria before delivery.

## Stage 6: Close and Renew

Record customer outcome, direct cost, gross margin, objections, reusable assets, next purchase trigger, and renewal owner. A project is not a case study until the customer permits publication or the case is fully anonymized.
