## Decision

- `Continue`: customer evidence and budget are sufficient for a bounded proposal.
- `Adjust`: problem exists but scope, payer, evidence, or delivery must change.
- `Pause`: interest exists but there is no budget or decision owner.
- `Stop`: demand is absent or the requested use is outside a safe/legal boundary.
