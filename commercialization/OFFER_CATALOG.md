# Offer Catalog

This catalog turns the public skills into paid, bounded offers. Prices are internal validation hypotheses and must be replaced by actual quotes and delivery-cost records.

## 1. Diagnostic Review

Best for a team that has a product idea but cannot identify a buyer, payer, budget, or next experiment.

Deliverables:

- customer segment and workflow map;
- pain, workaround, budget, and objection map;
- evidence ledger;
- customer-ROI hypothesis;
- next validation actions.

Acceptance: the customer can name the decision owner, the first testable use case, and the evidence still missing.

## 2. Commercial Validation Sprint

Best for a medical technology or rehabilitation product line preparing for market entry.

Deliverables:

- customer proof map;
- transaction and pricing matrix;
- buyer-side ROI;
- compliance and risk gates;
- 90-day paid-pilot plan;
- continue/adjust/pause/stop recommendation.

Acceptance: the customer receives a decision package that can be used to approve or reject a pilot.

## 3. Paid Pilot Design

Best for a hospital, rehabilitation institution, sports organization, school program, or chain operator that is ready to test a workflow.

Deliverables:

- scope and intended use;
- responsibilities and data boundaries;
- installation and service assumptions;
- success metrics;
- acceptance and payment milestones;
- record-and-replay review plan.

Acceptance: the parties can execute the pilot without relying on undefined claims, data rights, or service capacity.

## 4. Enterprise Operating Pack

Best for recurring commercial validation across several products, regions, or customer teams.

Deliverables:

- private templates and evidence ledger;
- role-based workflows;
- financial model and scenario controls;
- risk and KRI review;
- monthly or quarterly business review;
- training and annual updates.

## Pricing Formula

```text
price_floor = delivery_hours * loaded_hourly_cost
            + research_cost + compliance_review + software_cost
            + support_cost + travel + risk_reserve
price_proposal = price_floor / (1 - target_margin)
```

Do not publish a price as a market benchmark unless it has a traceable public source. Internal proposals must be labeled as hypotheses until validated by formal quotes, a paid pilot, and delivery records.
